# Reviewed role additions — 25 October 2026

OSRS Wiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. These exact-ID additions supplement the original catalogue; they do not replace activity equipment lists or imply that owning an item activates its effect.

- [Spade, revision 15380566](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Spade?oldid=15380566): retain the digging role in quest organization as well as Farming and clues.
- [Diving apparatus, revision 15381629](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Diving_apparatus?oldid=15381629) and [Fishbowl helmet, revision 15381630](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fishbowl_helmet?oldid=15381630): Sailing/diving requires both pieces and the relevant quest/access conditions. Neither individual piece is treated as an independent oxygen effect.
- [Prayer book, revision 15381288](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prayer_book?oldid=15381288): poison-curing utility for Hallowed Sepulchre, Pyramid Plunder and sulliusceps requires the book, a worn holy or hallowed symbol, and remaining Prayer points. The symbols retain their own identities and other uses. This is not poison immunity or an unconditional venom cure, and these roles do not add the items to raid supply lists.
- [Wyrmscraig boots, revision 15380555](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wyrmscraig_boots?oldid=15380555): decorative clothing; no combat recommendation is inferred from the equipment slot.

- [Blood money, revision 15380671](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Blood_money?oldid=15380671): retain currency membership and add the explicit Deadman restriction. Fairy tale quest lamp is explicitly unbankable and is not added.

The refresh checked revisions and category membership for all 12,521 item pages in the prior inventory. An unchanged article revision alone does not prove unchanged categories, because templates can add or remove categories. New item and guide evidence is retained in the development audit at `outputs/banking-reliability-audit-2026-10-25`.

## Unstrung bow correction — 26 October 2026

Twelve unstrung wooden bows inherit broad weapon categories on the Wiki, but their item pages explicitly mark them as unequipable Fletching inputs. Exact-ID role exclusions remove the combat and weapon-equipment labels while retaining Fletching, recipes and the Vale Totems decoration examples. This does not apply to upgraded Iban’s staff or uncharged Wilderness weapons merely because their names also end in `(u)`. Bank and GE searches share the correction.

## Placeholder-reference correction — 27 October 2026

[Spaniel puppy, revision 15344349](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Spaniel_puppy?oldid=15344349) lists 34566 for its second item variant. In the copied game cache this is a placeholder (template 14401) pointing to inventory item 34565, whose reciprocal placeholder is 34566. The exact inventory item now receives only the Companion pets role. The placeholder is not counted as owned, and no combat or activity benefit is inferred. RuneLite 1.13.4's generated ItemID identifies 34565 as SPANIEL_WHITE_PUPPY_OBJECT. The inconsistent Wiki colour labels are not used to infer further equivalences.
