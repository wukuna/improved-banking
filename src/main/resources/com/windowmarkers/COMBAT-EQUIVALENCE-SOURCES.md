# Scoped combat equivalents

OSRS Wiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Exact identities and pinned revisions below. Normal combat bonus tables were compared in full; matching numbers alone do not establish a shared effect. The named cape transformations were reviewed separately.

These aliases apply to combat preset membership and reviewed same-role rankings. They never transfer teleport destinations, clue eligibility, god protection, weight, death rules or every skillcape perk. Combined max capes retain Cooking/Crafting guild access, pouch protection and warmth, not the full ordinary max-cape perks. Dizana’s max cape inherits the blessed quiver; arrow/bolt-only Sunfire bonuses are not applied to other ammunition. Masori assembler retains assembler ammunition recovery. Broken and mangled states still require repair. Max-cape replacements require current real level 99 in every skill. Same-rank cosmetic variants are not worse items.
