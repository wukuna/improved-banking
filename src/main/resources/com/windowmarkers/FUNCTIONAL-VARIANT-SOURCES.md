# Functional variants and reduced-state guide rankings

OSRS Wiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Reviewed 2026-10-25. This supplement records the final 169 flagged exact variants in 30 item families, checked against current copied cache identities and the pinned sources below.

`activity-functional-equivalences.tsv` adds 104 directed alternatives to generic guide references: verified cosmetic blowpipes, serpentine helms, abyssal tentacles, Ahrim equipment, Venator bows, Iban staves, crystal armour and infernal tools, plus enhanced/cosmetic tridents. `catalogue-variant-notes.tsv` retains the distinction in bank and GE hover explanations. Exact version anchors and Treasure Trails requirements are not broadened. A full/charged requirement never becomes an empty or broken ready-to-use item.

Enhanced tridents retain their larger capacity. Ordinary-capacity tridents are not substituted for explicit enhanced requirements. Cosmetic crystal armour still needs charges and its appropriate bow partner. Echo Barrows retains durability states; zero durability never receives a ready tier.

The other reviewed family variants remain distinct: teleport charge counts, lantern log effects, recoil availability, quiver charge/blessing/max-cape effects, explicit charged/uncharged guide anchors, and filled/empty containers. A retained stat does not transfer every effect of a related item. Existing Fire/Infernal max-cape combat equivalences do not grant ordinary Max cape perks.

## Reduced function is separate from gear rank

Inactive crystal tools and uncharged infernal tools remain organizationally useful at their documented dragon tier, but do not inherit ranks for lost processing or gathering bonuses. The reviewed exceptions are the infernal axe's Wintertodt role and pickaxes in the CoX Guardians source tables, where the documented charge-dependent processing/gathering effect is irrelevant. The original guide conditions still apply.

Uncharged celestial rings/signets retain the Mining boost but do not inherit charged Mining recommendations; the celestial signet can retain its separate crystal-tool charge-saving role at Tempoross. Uncharged suffering rings do not establish recoil availability at Zulrah. Uncharged glories do not establish the gem-mining boost. Empty horns of plenty do not establish double catches. These cases stay searchable with neutral guide ranks and explicit state notes.

Damaged god books retain their expressly listed Prayer role; they do not acquire completed-book combat bonuses. An uncharged Ring of shadows retains its combat stats, not teleports. Those legitimate recommendations are not removed merely because the name contains damaged or uncharged.

A fresh Tempoross guide comparison added its Rune pouch and Astral/Fire/Water rune example. Existing saved guide IDs were preserved; the additional rune-pouch choice uses a new ID. Source quantities remain guide information rather than inferred owned supplies.
