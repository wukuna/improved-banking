# Item variant states

Identity evidence: OSRS Wiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Pinned revisions below. Policies are explicitly approved per family in work/build_variant_states.py; the remaining stateful pages are recorded separately and are not implicitly approved.

Broad organization retains depleted items. Activity suggestions put broken, unusable or role-ambiguous variants in unchecked review groups; they cannot satisfy sets or remove usable alternatives. Inactive crystal tools retain their dragon-tier use. Empty tomes retain stats, but are not presumed to supply runes or boosts. An uncharged glory retains combat stats, not teleports. Partial Barrows durability and partial trident charges remain usable. Corrupted crystal weapons continue using the existing permanent-charge alias. An ID never proves sufficient remaining charges, loaded ammunition or every player requirement; no charge counts are invented.
