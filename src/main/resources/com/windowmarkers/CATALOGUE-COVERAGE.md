# Improved Banking item audit

## Audited snapshot

Reviewed through 2026-10-25. All 12,521 direct Wiki Category:Items pages in the collected snapshot have a recorded disposition. Raw articles, revision IDs, exact item definitions, category rules and exceptions are retained in the development audit ledger. This is a completed reconciliation of the available snapshot, not a claim that every possible OSRS item or use has been proven.

There are 13,633 potentially bankable, literal, current-source identities that agree with the copied game cache. 13,632 have exact organizational roles. Reef snake gland (32882) has no verified functional use in its cache-derived Wiki article, so it stays in an unchecked review group. Bankability marked unknown by the source is not silently promoted to proven bankability.

Across the earlier catalogue and the new supplements, the compiled tables contain 13,887 distinct exact IDs and 35,713 memberships after explicit identity and role exclusions. These counts overlap the source and category counts; they must not be added. Some records describe retained seasonal or historical objects. Those do not establish activity eligibility.

## Classification and equivalent effects

General organization combines explicit Wiki categories, item facts and 4,932 recipe records. Recipe inputs and tools map to 2,824 exact IDs. All recipe input records were resolved or given an explicit disposition: spells, reward points, empty fields, processing fluids, tool classes and non-bank/activity-only materials are not invented as inventory objects.

Combat equivalence is scoped to combat roles. Fire max/Fire cape, Infernal max/Infernal cape, assembler, quiver and god-cape variants retain their distinct utility and requirements. The reviewed cape rules cover 42 exact IDs, with 254 additional exact IDs from 181 verified cosmetic families. Identical bonus tables alone do not transfer teleports, clue identity, god affiliation, passive effects or Max cape perks. An ornamented fortified ward maps to the fortified ward, not the ordinary ward. Imbued Slayer helmets stay distinct from unimbued helmets; poison states remain distinct. Equal-ranked alternatives are retained.

There are 26 separately scoped skilling-effect relations across 178 IDs. Spirit angler pieces share the ordinary angler XP effect without making ordinary pieces satisfy spirit tethering. Golden prospector and Forestry variants retain their documented XP benefits. Graceful recolours and the appropriate Agility/ordinary Max cape movement substitutions are restricted to skilling contexts. Combat Max cape variants do not inherit those movement substitutions. Clues and unrelated uses keep exact identities.

## States, restrictions and replacements

Explicit state policies from 569 reviewed records cover 516 distinct normalized families and 1,600 IDs, all checked against the copied cache. Every discovered state candidate has a policy or source-backed non-banking disposition. Broken/depleted equipment remains available for organization, but cannot complete a usable set or remove usable alternatives. Direct category search preserves the same per-state notes as auto-categorization. Partial Barrows durability remains usable; zero durability needs repair. Crystal, Moon, trident, quiver and ornament versions keep their separate state.

Reduced function is not treated as universal uselessness. Inactive crystal and depleted infernal tools retain their documented basic tool use. An uncharged glory retains combat stats but loses its teleports and gem-mining benefit. Charged, trimmed and eternal glories retain that mining role. Containers and hidden counters do not prove their exact contents or remaining charges. Requirements, consumable amounts, active prayers/spellbook/invocations and equipped set completeness are not inferred from bank ownership.

Exclude worse items retains its conservative, supported CoX/ToB/ToA comparisons and ordinary tool tiers. It preserves specialist uses, special attacks, set-dependent gear, unknown eligibility and equal alternatives. It is not a universal gear optimizer and is not applied to the additional source setup examples.

## Activity guides

888 equipment tables, example inventories and rune-pouch examples from 106 activities are available through the searchable Wiki activity/setup choice. Each is kept separate, with source tab/column names, a pinned revision link and conditional notes. All start unchecked. Generic or unresolved references remain visible in the notes instead of being filled from broad categories. All 167 previously unresolved markup/condition fields now have revision-bound interpretations. Ordinary linked alternatives, named footnotes, skill requirements and page variables are resolved; weapon/set dependencies and cross-section choices remain explicit conditions, not a universal ranking.

CoX/ToB/ToA retain their main presets and separate specialist roles. Additional covered guide contexts include Tekton crush, Vasa stab, Vespula fire-spell conditions, ToB Entry alternatives and Verzik P1 multi-hit weapons, and ToA Kephri/core roles. These now have explicit memberships without replacing the main-role comparisons. The Fractured Archive source is under construction and remains provisional and unchecked. No raid admits supplies merely because an item has an Eat or Drink action.

## 25 October refresh and remaining work

All 12,521 item-page revisions and category lists were checked again, separately. There were 89 revised articles, 11 changed category lists and no new or removed pages in this direct-category inventory. None of the changed item articles altered the exact identity, variant-name or bankability fields used by the identity ledger. Of 114 source guide pages, 14 changed; their setup tables and conditions were rebuilt. The current 888 setup choices contain 58,833 exact-ID memberships across 2,432 distinct items after the functional-variant and Tempoross follow-up.

Reviewed role additions cover the diving pair, conditional prayer-book poison curing, quest digging, decorative Wyrmscraig boots, Deadman currency and the new crossbow/crush weapons. Eleven explicit reference-group definitions cover books, capes, halos, blessings, shields and specialist weapons without turning their effects into universal equivalence. Kraken damage and sustain setups stay separate, with the sustain shield requirement visible. See WIKI-ROLE-UPDATES.md and ACTIVITY-SETUP-SOURCES.md.

All 15 previously broad/contextual reference names now have explicit interpretations for their reviewed guide fields. Thirteen context rules cover 89 fields, including Barrows armour slots, prayer/defence capes, powered staves, crossbows and Scorching-bow ammunition. The missing stone-tablet title is resolved using the exact image/variant mapping in its item infobox. Five names remain intentionally unexpanded: Mimic, Logavano seed, Moonlight potion and Bloated toad are unbankable; Placeholder is a diagram spacer. The 167 conditional fields were then reviewed individually: 71 include additional literal alternatives, two require an upgraded granite maul, and 16 expand a Vale Totems offering variable. Five cross-section references remain player choices. Conditional notes are retained rather than treated as automatically verified eligibility.

Redirect targets now retain their specific versions (such as lantern fuel, cape trim, poison and doses). Explicit versions only gain reviewed cosmetic alternatives with the same version. Broad family choices retain depleted/broken forms as review items. Three additional ornament relations cover the holy Sanguinesti staff and holy/sanguine Scythes of Vitur: combat roles are shared, but charge state and Saradomin affiliation are separate. This brings reviewed cosmetic combat relations to 181, covering 254 exact IDs.

Reef snake gland's function remains unverified. The 593 named cache entries without matched current literal Wiki identities also remain guarded; those are not 593 proven missing bankable items. Further guide interpretation, new source content and live account-specific readiness remain open work. This audit does not claim every possible item use or every preset's optimality is complete.

## Identity exceptions and safe handling

The cache contains 34,732 definitions, including notes, placeholders, interface icons, obsolete and internal entries. 593 named entries have no matching current literal Wiki identity even after exact-title article checks. They remain explicitly unverified, not declared unbankable. If encountered with that identity and name, they cannot become automatically selected raid gear or normal broad-category items merely by sharing a familiar name.

All 4,331 earlier exact catalogue IDs were reconciled. 39 stale, unbankable or conflicting identities are prevented from regaining memberships through old name aliases. Other shared display names use exact-ID evidence. Notes/placeholders are not imported as ordinary identities. An interface-page ID typo cannot override a verified inventory identity.

No known, cache-matching current-source identity is left without either an exact organizational role or an explicit unknown-function disposition. Source gaps, future items, undocumented uses and account-specific readiness remain visible limitations. This does not establish that every bankable item or every possible use is known.

## Optional Exclude worse items

Unchecked by default. Filters new suggestions only, with all exclusions shown before confirmation. Compares ordinary bronze through dragon axes/pickaxes owned in the bank against actual Mining/Woodcutting levels. It preserves known clue/quest uses, black pickaxes for low-weight uses, and special tools such as crystal, infernal, felling, ornamented and gilded variants. Raid presets also compare the Wiki-ranked alternatives within the same combat role and equipment slot when wearing requirements are known and met. Set-dependent and specialist items, supplies, ties and unknown eligibility remain. Comparisons do not use prices or bonus totals. See RAID-FILTER-SOURCES.md for requirement-page revisions. It does not delete items or existing markers, and it does not use temporary boosts to infer eligibility.
