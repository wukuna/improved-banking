## Consolidated activity views

The catalogue presents one entry per activity, retaining exact source tables for guide selection, saved-view contexts and evidence. Separate phase/style equipment and inventory layouts are stacked inside that activity. Inventories remain distinct 28-position source examples; they are not merged. Weapon-only phase tables can inherit compatible equipment, while ordinary room/tool lists remain grouped alternatives.

Omitted or unavailable ordinary slots are completed from compatible same-activity equipment, followed by the shared ToA combat-style equipment choices. The latter are explicitly labelled owned alternatives, not new boss-specific rankings. A listed main weapon remains method-defining; it is not replaced with another method. Required wyvern/basilisk shields, Shaman protection and smoke-devil head protection retain their source choices. Unavailable slots show a labelled placeholder; two-handed weapons identify the blocked off-hand.

Avernic completion distinguishes the relevant upgrade from base/non-matching upgrades. Base treads may fill a missing slot but are not promoted to the upgraded tier. Rada's blessing 4 ownership establishes its diary unlock; this exception does not bypass unrelated task, quest or equipment requirements. Required arrows and bolts still take priority over blessings.
