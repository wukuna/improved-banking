# Shared bank / Grand Exchange discovery and guide tiers

OSRS Wiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Derived data reviewed 2026-10-25.

The GE index shares the bundled item categories and the 858 activity setups for 105 activities documented in [ACTIVITY-SETUP-SOURCES.md](ACTIVITY-SETUP-SOURCES.md). Only identities reported GE-tradeable by the running client are exposed as purchase results. Notes, placeholders and unavailable variants are excluded. Purchasable alternatives use the explicit, source-backed links below; the index does not guess replacements from similar names or transfer an untradeable item's effects to a tradeable base item.

`activity-setup-ranks.tsv` preserves the equipment slot and numbered column of the same revision-bound fields resolved by the activity compiler. Its 48,547 rows cover 321 recommended-equipment tables in 97 activities. Example inventories, example equipment and ordinary category membership do not supply ranks. Fields beginning with “None”, “N/A” or “No offhand” describe an empty slot; items linked in those conditions are not ranked as equipment for that slot.

The [Recommended equipment module, revision 15325719](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Module:Recommended_equipment?oldid=15325719) describes its columns as most effective to least effective and renders numbered slot parameters in ascending order. See also the [template documentation, revision 15309548](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Template:Recommended_equipment/doc?oldid=15309548). Colours represent source columns 1, 2 and 3 within the matching setup and role; unavailable higher choices never promote a lower column. They are not a global item-stat, price or account-specific BIS ranking.

Within-cell ordered alternatives use column 0 (review needed) rather than treating differently ordered choices as tied. Conflicting columns across matching setups or across ordinary/special-attack roles stay neutral. Unknown identities and items needing charge, repair, preparation or other state review also stay neutral. Hover explanations retain field conditions; the Views guide picker opens the pinned source and lets the player narrow an activity to one table. Guide requirements, complete set bonuses, active charges and skill levels are not inferred.

`ge-item-slots.tsv` contains 5,987 exact identities derived from current-cache names and Wiki item-page slot categories in the existing item-definition audit, including Weapon and Hands categories. It is a fallback for variants without RuneLite equipment stats, including some uncharged purchase states. A changed item name invalidates that fallback. It does not transfer combat effectiveness or usability from the wearable variant.

`ge-purchase-variants.tsv` supplies ten directed links with exact target-name guards and pinned sources. They add the uncharged GE forms of Craw's bow and Webweaver bow to their charged recommendations, enhanced-capacity tridents to ordinary trident recommendations, an empty watering can to the filled-can recommendation, and six-charge/uncharged combat bracelets to a four-charge example inventory. These explicit discovery links are shared by bank search and GE search; owned bank identities and GE-tradeable identities remain separate result pools. They do not invent a ready-to-use rank for a purchase state. Enhanced tridents keep their greater capacity distinction; uncharged bows and tridents, empty cans and teleport jewellery retain preparation/charge explanations. An ordinary-capacity trident is not substituted for an enhanced-capacity requirement.

`ge-functional-slots.tsv` records three exact, source-backed roles: Dinh's bulwark is a two-handed shield, and dragon/amethyst darts can be blowpipe ammunition. Their search roles supplement their physical weapon slot. A tier must still come from the matching Wiki slot: a shield recommendation does not become a weapon tier, or ammunition a thrown-weapon tier. The two-handed and ammunition conditions are retained in hover explanations. Other combination ingredients and links do not inherit an equipment rank.

Purchase/role sources are pinned in the corresponding TSV rows, including [enhanced trident](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Trident_of_the_Seas_(e)?oldid=15316738), [enhanced toxic trident](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Trident_of_the_Swamp_(e)?oldid=15317835), [Craw's bow](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Craw%27s_bow?oldid=15371542), [Webweaver bow](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Webweaver_bow?oldid=15309891), [watering can](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Watering_can?oldid=15366081), [combat bracelet](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Combat_bracelet?oldid=15320281), [Dinh's bulwark](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dinh%27s_bulwark?oldid=15369281), [dragon dart](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dragon_dart?oldid=15372145) and [amethyst dart](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Amethyst_dart?oldid=15301666).

Implementation uses `GrandExchangeSearched` and the public GE result setters, then decorates the native result label widgets after `ScriptID.GE_ITEM_SEARCH`. It respects previously consumed searches and leaves unrecognized queries to native search or RuneLite's fuzzy search. It never edits offers, prices, quantities, item actions or chatbox input. The 250-result native limit is retained; broad queries must be narrowed.

Category matching prefers existing exact words and prefixes. If those find no category, a unique nearest complete-word correction takes priority over loose fuzzy prefixes, so `minign tools` resolves to mining instead of also matching minigame tools. Ambiguous corrections fall back to the existing bounded fuzzy matching; ordinary exact item names remain native searches.

The bank and GE use the same immutable category, slot and guide-rank index. Native bank searches, reviewed setup groups and saved setup views use the same purple/1st, blue/2nd and green/3rd presentation as the GE. Saved groups retain their guide context; editing membership into a manual custom group removes the inherited guide context. Mixed/conflicting guide roles remain neutral. Colour settings are shared under Gear guide tiers; the GE search enable setting retains its existing storage key and defaults off.

The final family reconciliation and charge-dependent ranking decisions are documented in [FUNCTIONAL-VARIANT-SOURCES.md](FUNCTIONAL-VARIANT-SOURCES.md). Cosmetic aliases add exact identities, not generic name-substring matches.
